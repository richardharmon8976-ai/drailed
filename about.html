About Us

Durand's Favorite Local Hangout

D-Railed Bar & Grill is Durand's go-to casual tavern for comfort food done right, cold drinks, and an atmosphere that keeps people coming back. Whether you're grabbing a bite after work or catching a live band on the weekend, we've got you covered.

From legendary Flint-style coney dogs to loaded taco salads and the best fish dinner around, our kitchen delivers the real stuff. Pair it with happy hour deals and you've got yourself a night to remember.
